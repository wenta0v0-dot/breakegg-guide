Break and Steal an Egg: Beginner Guide

Last updated October 12, 2026 · written for brand-new players

Break and Steal an Egg is a heist wrapped in a pet collector. You hatch rare animals out of eggs, steal them from other players, escape the guards, and park your loot in a base that prints cash. This page explains the whole loop and where your first cash should go.

The core loop

  1. Break eggs. Smash eggs out in the world — the rarer the egg, the rarer the animal that hatches out of it.
  2. Hatch & carry. Hatched animals are yours to carry. The rarer the animal, the more it's worth — and the more everyone else wants it.
  3. Steal & escape. Other players' bases are your shopping aisle. Grab an animal, then outrun the guards and the owner before the carry timer runs out.
  4. Bank it. Place animals in your own base. They generate cash per second while they sit there — that's your income engine.
  5. Upgrade & repeat. Spend cash on Speed, Pickaxes, Trails and Pen capacity, then go break, hatch and steal bigger targets.

Zones you'll run into

The map is split into zones, each with its own egg pool and guards. Higher zones hold rarer eggs — and meanier defenses.

ZoneWhat it's for
ForestWhere everyone starts. Common eggs, gentle guards, your first few animals.
DesertThe step up — better eggs, longer chases. Bring a Speed upgrade.
Galaxy High tierRare egg territory. Expect other players to be hunting here too.

More zones have been added in updates — this list is re-checked after every patch, and the full zone-by-zone egg tables are coming to the pets & eggs database.

Your first upgrades, in order

UpgradeWhy it matters
SpeedThe whole game is a chase. Speed wins steals and escapes — buy this before anything flashy.
PickaxeA better pickaxe breaks eggs faster, which means more hatches per run.
Pen capacityMore animals parked in your base = more cash per second while you're out stealing.
TrailsMostly cosmetic flex — buy it when your income is already rolling.

Five beginner mistakes (don't)

  • Don't hoard cash. Cash sitting in your wallet earns nothing; animals parked in your pen earn every second. Convert early.
  • Don't steal before you upgrade Speed. The #1 way new players lose animals is getting chased down.
  • Don't ignore the group rewards. Liking the game and joining the group is free money — see the rewards page for the details.
  • Don't chase the rarest egg on day one. A pen full of common animals out-earns one rare animal you lost mid-heist.
  • Don't trust code lists. The game has no text codes — anyone selling you "working codes" is wasting your time. Here's the proof.
