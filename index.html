What is Break and Steal an Egg?

It's one of the fastest-growing games on Roblox right now. The loop is simple and very stealable-of-your-attention: you break open eggs to hatch rare animals, sneak them past guards — or steal other players' animals — and place them in your base, where they earn cash over time. Cash buys upgrades: faster movement, better pickaxes, and more space for your collection.

You can play it safe and hatch your own, or play the heist and rob everyone else. Both routes feed the same goal: filling your index with every animal in the game.
