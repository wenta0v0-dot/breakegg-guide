That also means any site or video promising "ALL WORKING CODES" for this game is wrong in one of two ways: the list was scraped from a different Roblox game, or it is bait for views. Neither one can hand you a reward here.

1. Open the official **Break and Steal an Egg** page on Roblox and join its Discord server through the community link.
