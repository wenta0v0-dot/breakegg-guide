Break and Steal an Egg is a heist wrapped in a pet collector. You hatch rare animals out of eggs, steal them from other players, escape the guards, and park your loot in a base that prints cash. This guide explains the whole loop, the zones you will run into, and where your first cash should go — written for brand-new players.

## The core loop, step by step

Every session is the same five beats, and each one feeds the next:

1. **Break eggs.** Smash eggs out in the world — the rarer the egg, the rarer the animal that hatches out of it.
2. **Hatch & carry.** Hatched animals are yours to carry. The rarer the animal, the more it is worth — and the more everyone else wants it.
3. **Steal & escape.** Other players' bases are your shopping aisle. Grab an animal, then outrun the guards and the owner before the carry timer runs out.
4. **Bank it.** Place animals in your own base. They generate cash per second while they sit there — that is your income engine.
5. **Upgrade & repeat.** Spend cash on Speed, Pickaxes, Trails and Pen capacity, then go break, hatch and steal bigger targets.

<Callout type="tip" title="The one-line strategy">
  A pen full of common animals out-earns one rare animal you lost mid-heist. Bank early, steal often, upgrade Speed first.
</Callout>

## Zones you'll run into

The map is split into zones, each with its own egg pool and guards. Higher zones hold rarer eggs — and meaner defenses.

| Zone | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| Forest | Where everyone starts. Common eggs, gentle guards, your first few animals. |
| Desert | The step up — better eggs, longer chases. Bring a Speed upgrade. |
| Galaxy | Rare egg territory. Expect other players to be hunting here too. |

More zones have been added in updates — this list is re-checked after every patch, and the full zone-by-zone egg tables are planned for the pets database once the data can be verified properly.

## Your first upgrades, in order

Cash is only useful the moment you spend it. This is the order that pays for itself fastest:

| Upgrade | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Speed | The whole game is a chase. Speed wins steals *and* escapes — buy this before anything flashy. |
| Pickaxe | A better pickaxe breaks eggs faster, which means more hatches per run. |
| Pen capacity | More animals parked in your base = more cash per second while you're out stealing. |
| Trails | Mostly cosmetic flex — buy it when your income is already rolling. |

## Five beginner mistakes (don't)

- **Don't hoard cash.** Cash sitting in your wallet earns nothing; animals parked in your pen earn every second. Convert early.
- **Don't steal before you upgrade Speed.** The number one way new players lose animals is getting chased down.
- **Don't ignore the group rewards.** Liking the game and joining the group is free money — the [free rewards page](/codes/all-codes/) has the exact steps.
- **Don't chase the rarest egg on day one.** A pen full of common animals out-earns one rare animal you lost mid-heist.
- **Don't trust code lists.** The game has no text codes — anyone selling you "working codes" is wasting your time. [Here's the proof](/codes/all-codes/).
